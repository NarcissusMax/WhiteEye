## Define a role from the work before hiring
QUESTION: What work is missing, and which authority and support would let someone do it?
CORE: Hiring can become a reaction to overload without a clear role. Identify the recurring work, its expected output, skills required, decision rights, training, and cost. Compare hiring with redesigning or reducing the work. A title alone does not tell a candidate what success or support will look like. Real employment decisions require current local law and fair process; a general business book is a planning aid, not a legal checklist.
CONNECT: [[22 Planning, collaboration, and delivery]] identifies capacity and handoffs. [[19 Leadership, teams, and institutions]] matches authority with accountability.
EXAMPLE: A small service says it needs an assistant, then discovers it needs someone to manage bookings and customer follow-up with access to a defined system.
DO: Write a one-page role brief for a hypothetical small business with tasks, authority, support, and expected results.
TRY: Compare the role with one non-hiring change to the process.
REFLECT: Was I trying to fill a title or solve a clear capacity problem?
CHECK: What should be defined before hiring?::The work, output, authority, support, cost, and fair conditions of the role.
