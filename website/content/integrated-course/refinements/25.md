## Separate near-term resilience from distant wealth goals
QUESTION: Which money needs to be available soon, and which goal can tolerate a longer horizon?
CORE: A distant wealth target and an upcoming bill solve different problems. List obligations by timing and consequences, then decide what must remain accessible before taking longer-term risks. Avoid using a high-income image as evidence of financial security. The size and form of any buffer depend on circumstances; this lesson asks for the distinction, not a universal number or investment choice. Review it when income, dependents, or commitments change.
