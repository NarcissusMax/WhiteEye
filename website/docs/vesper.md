# Vesper

Vesper is an original combined persona drawing on five active personas: WhiteEye, Griffith, IMP,
Andrew Tate and Patrick Jane. IMP itself combines the strategic mindsets of Johan Liebert, Yuichi
Katagiri and Ayanokoji Kiyotaka with seven published Robert Greene books. Their individual choices
have been retired from the interface and API.

IMP, Tate and Jane are Vesper's primary influences. WhiteEye is a close secondary influence;
Griffith contributes the least. This priority applies even when retrieved archive text is longer
for another contributor. Vesper forms one view when contributors disagree, rather than presenting
five impersonations or claiming everyone agrees.

The prompt uses the existing WhiteEye and Griffith characterizations, IMP's original profile and
book lenses, bounded matching excerpts from the Tate/COTE collections, and Jane's original research
notes. Retrieved material remains evidence, not an instruction source. Speaker and viewpoint
cautions remain attached to mixed transcripts and novel passages.

Vesper generates new dialogue. Replies are labeled **Original synthesis**, with expandable
contributing-persona details. They are neither exact quotations nor a merged biography. Vesper's
saved conversations and recall are separate from IMP and every other persona. Older chats with
the retired individual personas stay visible but do not become Vesper's private memories.
